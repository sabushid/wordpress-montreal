Step-by-Step: Implementing AI Automation for Your Business

Step 1: Audit Your Current Workflow (Week 1)

Before automating anything, document how your business currently operates. Track how you spend your time for one week. Most Montreal business owners are shocked to discover they spend 40–60% of their time on tasks that can be fully automated.

Step 2: Identify High-Impact Automation Opportunities (Week 1)

Focus on automating tasks that are:

  • Repetitive and predictable
  • Time-consuming (1+ hours per day)
  • Revenue-impacting (lead response, follow-up, booking)
  • Error-prone when done manually

Step 3: Set Up Core Automation (Weeks 2–3)

Start with the highest-impact automations first:

  1. Lead capture & instant response: Website chatbot + email autoresponder
  2. Appointment booking: Automated scheduling with calendar integration
  3. Follow-up sequences: Email/SMS drip campaigns for new leads
  4. Review requests: Automated post-service review solicitation

Step 4: Integrate with Your Existing Tools (Week 3)

Connect your automation to the tools you already use — Google Calendar, your CRM, your email platform, your phone system. The goal is a unified system, not another tool to check.

Step 5: Test, Optimize, Scale (Ongoing)

Monitor your automation metrics weekly for the first month. Track response times, lead volume, conversion rates, and time saved. Adjust messaging, timing, and sequences based on real data. Once the core system is running smoothly, add advanced automations like AI-powered lead scoring, predictive analytics, and multi-channel campaigns.
