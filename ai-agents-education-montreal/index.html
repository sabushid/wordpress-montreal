FAQ

Got Questions?

What AI tools will I learn in the workshops?+

You'll get hands-on experience with ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, Google Gemini, automation platforms like Make and Zapier, and AI-powered business tools tailored to your Montréal industry.

Do I need technical experience to attend?+

Not at all. Our workshops are designed for small business owners and teams with zero AI experience. We start from the basics and build up to advanced applications.

How long are the training sessions?+

Our intro session is 60 minutes and free. Full workshops run 3-4 hours, and our intensive course is a 2-day program. All sessions include hands-on exercises with your own business scenarios.

Can you train my entire team?+

Yes. We offer private team training sessions at your Montréal office or virtually. Teams of 3-25 people, customized to your industry and workflow.

What is prompt engineering and why does it matter?+

Prompt engineering is the skill of writing effective instructions for AI tools. A well-crafted prompt can turn a 2-hour task into a 5-minute task. We teach you the frameworks top Montréal businesses use.

Will I be able to build automations after the training?+

Absolutely. By the end of our automation workshop, you'll have built at least 2-3 working automations for your business -- email workflows, content pipelines, or data analysis tools.

Are the workshops available in French?+

Oui! All our workshops and courses are available in both English and French. Bilingual materials included for Montréal teams.

Which Montréal neighbourhoods do you serve?+

We deliver training across the entire island -- Plateau Mont-Royal, Griffintown, Ville-Marie, NDG, Westmount, Verdun, Rosemont, Mile End, and all Montréal boroughs. On-site or virtual.

Is the intro session really free?+

Yes, 100% free with no commitment. It's a 60-minute session where we assess your business needs and show you quick AI wins you can implement immediately.

How quickly will I see results after training?+

Most Montréal business owners start saving 5-10 hours per week within the first week after training. Some teams report 40-60% productivity gains within 30 days.

Do you offer ongoing support after the workshop?+

Yes. Every participant gets 30 days of post-workshop email support, access to our resource library, and priority booking for advanced sessions.

What industries benefit most from AI training?+

Every industry benefits. We've trained Montréal teams in real estate, retail, restaurants, professional services, healthcare, e-commerce, marketing agencies, and more. AI tools are universal productivity multipliers.
