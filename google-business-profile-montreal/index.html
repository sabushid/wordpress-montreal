FAQ

Got Questions?

What is Google Business Profile optimization for real estate agents?+

Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization ensures your real estate profile appears at the top of Google Maps when buyers and sellers in Montréal search for agents. It includes profile setup, category selection, photo optimization, review strategy, and local citation building.

How long does it take to rank on Google Maps in Montréal?+

Most Montréal real estate agents see significant improvements in their Maps ranking within 4–8 weeks. Competitive neighbourhoods like Plateau and Ville-Marie may take slightly longer, but our proven process delivers consistent results.

Do I need a physical office to have a Google Business Profile?+

No. Real estate agents can use a Service Area Business (SAB) profile on Google, which shows your service area without displaying a physical address. This is perfect for agents who work from home or meet clients at properties across Montréal.

How do Google reviews help my real estate business?+

Google reviews are the #1 ranking factor for local Maps results. Agents with more 5-star reviews rank higher, attract more clicks, and build instant trust. Our review management system automates the process so you get consistent reviews from every happy client.

What are local citations and why do they matter?+

Local citations are online mentions of your business name, address, and phone number (NAP) across directories like Centris, Realtor.ca, Yelp, and Pages Jaunes. Consistent citations signal trust to Google and help your GBP rank higher in Montréal Maps results.

Can you manage my GBP for multiple neighbourhoods?+

Yes. We optimize your GBP to target multiple Montréal neighbourhoods including Plateau, Griffintown, Ville-Marie, NDG, Westmount, Verdun, Rosemont, and Mile End. Our strategy ensures you appear for local searches across your entire service area.

How much does GBP optimization cost for Montreal agents?+

Our GBP optimization packages start with a free audit. Pricing depends on your goals — whether you need basic optimization, review management, or a full local SEO package with citations. We offer flexible month-to-month plans with no long-term contracts.

Will I see how many calls and direction requests come from my GBP?+

Absolutely. Google Business Profile provides built-in analytics showing your Maps views, search queries, phone calls, direction requests, and website clicks. We provide monthly reports so you can see exactly how your profile is performing.

What if I already have a Google Business Profile?+

Most Montréal agents have a GBP but it's not optimized. Our free audit analyzes your current profile, identifies gaps, and shows you exactly what's keeping you off the Maps 3-pack. We then optimize every element for maximum visibility.

Do you handle GBP for both English and French searches?+

Yes. Montréal is bilingual, so we optimize your GBP for both English and French search queries. This means you appear when someone searches "real estate agent Montreal" or "courtier immobilier Montréal" on Google Maps.

How is GBP optimization different from regular SEO?+

Regular SEO focuses on ranking your website in organic search results. GBP optimization focuses on ranking in the Google Maps 3-pack — the map results that appear above organic results for local searches. For real estate agents, the Maps 3-pack drives the most calls and direction requests.

Is the GBP audit really free?+

Yes, 100% free with no commitment. We'll audit your current Google Business Profile, analyze your competitors in the Montréal market, and show you a clear roadmap to rank in the Maps 3-pack. Then you decide if you want to work with us.
