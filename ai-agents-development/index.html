FAQ

Got Questions?

What is an AI agent?+

An AI agent is an automated software assistant that can handle tasks like answering customer questions, qualifying leads, booking appointments, and processing data — 24/7, without human intervention. Think of it as a tireless digital employee for your business.

How long does it take to build a custom AI agent?+

Most AI agents are designed, built, and deployed within 1–2 weeks depending on complexity. Simple customer service chatbots can be live in as little as 3–5 business days.

Do I need technical skills to use an AI agent?+

Not at all. We build and configure everything for you. Your AI agent works autonomously once deployed, and we provide a simple dashboard so you can monitor performance without any coding knowledge.

What tasks can an AI agent automate for my business?+

AI agents can automate customer service (FAQ, live chat), lead qualification and follow-up, appointment booking, data entry, invoicing, email responses, scheduling, and workflow coordination between your existing tools.

How much does a custom AI agent cost?+

Our AI agents cost a fraction of hiring an employee — typically 60–80% less than a part-time staff member. Pricing depends on complexity, but most small businesses invest between $1,500–$5,000 for setup with affordable monthly maintenance.

Can the AI agent integrate with my existing tools?+

Yes. We integrate with popular tools including Google Workspace, Calendly, HubSpot, Shopify, QuickBooks, Slack, WordPress, and hundreds of others via API connections and Zapier.

Will the AI agent work in both French and English?+

Absolutely. All our AI agents are built to be fully bilingual (French and English), which is essential for serving customers in Montreal and Longueuil.

What happens if the AI agent can't answer a question?+

The agent gracefully escalates to a human team member via email, SMS, or Slack notification. It collects the customer's details and context so your team can follow up quickly with full information.

Is my business data safe with an AI agent?+

Yes. We follow strict data privacy practices compliant with Canadian privacy laws (PIPEDA) and Quebec's Law 25. Your data is encrypted, never shared with third parties, and stored on secure Canadian servers.

Do you offer ongoing support and updates?+

Yes. We offer monthly support plans that include performance monitoring, conversation analysis, agent retraining, and feature updates to keep your AI agent improving over time.

Can I try the AI agent before committing?+

Yes. We offer a free consultation where we demo a prototype tailored to your business. You see exactly how the agent would work before making any commitment.

What industries do you serve in Montreal and Longueuil?+

We serve all types of small businesses — restaurants, dental clinics, law firms, salons, contractors, accountants, gyms, retail stores, medical clinics, and agencies across Montreal, Longueuil, and the Rive-Sud.
