FAQ

Got Questions?

Do I need technical skills or coding experience to attend?+

Not at all. Our workshops are designed for non-technical business owners and teams. No coding required -- we teach you to use AI tools through simple interfaces and plain language.

What AI tools do you teach?+

We cover ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Midjourney, automation platforms like Make and Zapier, and business-specific tools. We always teach the latest and most relevant tools for your industry.

How long are the workshops?+

Our AI Fundamentals Workshop is a 1-day intensive. The Automation Bootcamp is 2 days. Ongoing coaching is monthly sessions. All formats are designed to fit busy business schedules.

Are the workshops in-person or online?+

Both. We offer in-person workshops in Montreal and Longueuil, plus virtual options for teams who prefer remote learning. Same curriculum, same hands-on approach.

Do you train entire teams?+

Yes. Team training is one of our most popular services. We customize the curriculum to your business needs and train your entire team together so everyone is on the same page.

What size groups do you work with?+

From 1-on-1 coaching to groups of 20+. Small groups (4-8 people) are our sweet spot for hands-on workshops. For larger teams, we adapt the format with breakout sessions.

How much does AI training cost?+

Pricing depends on format and group size. We offer a free intro session so you can experience our teaching style before committing. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your needs.

Do participants receive a certificate?+

Yes. Every participant receives a Rushanet AI Training certificate upon completion, plus access to our resource library and community for ongoing support.

What if my team resists learning AI?+

This is one of the most common challenges we solve. Our approach shows teams that AI helps them do their jobs better -- not replace them. We start with quick wins that build confidence and excitement.

Can you create a custom training program for my business?+

Absolutely. We assess your business needs, identify high-impact AI opportunities, and build a training program around your specific workflows, tools, and goals.

Is the training available in French?+

Yes -- all our training is available in both French and English. Our bilingual trainers deliver workshops in whichever language your team prefers.

What results can I expect after training?+

Most participants start using AI tools within the first week. On average, trained employees save 5-10 hours per week on repetitive tasks. Many businesses see ROI within the first month.
