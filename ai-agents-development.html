FAQ

Got Questions?

What is a custom AI agent?+

A custom AI agent is a purpose-built software system that uses artificial intelligence to perform specific tasks for your business — such as following up with leads, answering customer questions, booking appointments, or automating workflows — without human intervention.

How long does it take to build an AI agent?+

Most AI agents are built and deployed within 1-3 weeks depending on complexity. Simple agents like lead follow-up or appointment booking can be live in as little as 5 business days.

Do I need technical skills to use the AI agent?+

Not at all. We handle all the technical work — design, development, integration, and deployment. You get a fully working agent with a simple dashboard to monitor performance. We also provide training and ongoing support.

What systems can your AI agents integrate with?+

Our AI agents integrate with all major platforms including CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce), email providers, SMS services, phone systems, calendars, Slack, Zapier, and custom APIs. If your tool has an API, we can connect to it.

How much does a custom AI agent cost?+

Pricing depends on complexity and integrations. Most projects range from $2,000 to $10,000 for initial development. The ROI typically pays for itself within 30-60 days through time savings and increased revenue.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?+

Yes. Every AI agent comes with 30 days of free post-launch support. We also offer monthly optimization plans to continuously improve your agent's performance, add new capabilities, and ensure it stays aligned with your evolving business needs.
