FAQ

Got Questions?

How does the automated listing post work?+

When you add a new listing, our system automatically generates a professional post with photos, details, and neighbourhood tags — then publishes it to your Facebook and Instagram accounts instantly.

Do I need to create any content myself?+

No. We handle everything — listing posts, neighbourhood spotlights, market stat carousels, Reels, and agent branding content. You just approve and we publish.

Which platforms do you cover?+

We cover Facebook and Instagram — the two platforms where Montreal and Longueuil real estate buyers and sellers are most active. We handle feed posts, Stories, and Reels.

Can you create Reels and video content?+

Yes. We create neighbourhood spotlight Reels from listing photos and local footage, market update videos, and agent branding clips optimized for Instagram and Facebook Reels.

How does retargeting work?+

We install Facebook and Instagram pixels on your website. When visitors browse listings, they see your ads on their social feeds featuring the exact properties they viewed — bringing them back to your site.

Do you manage my existing social accounts?+

Yes. We work with your existing Facebook and Instagram business accounts. If you don't have them set up properly, we'll create and optimize them as part of onboarding.

What areas do you cover?+

We serve real estate agents across Montreal (Plateau, Griffintown, Ville-Marie, NDG, Westmount, Rosemont) and Longueuil (Vieux-Longueuil, Brossard, Saint-Hubert, Saint-Lambert, and the entire Rive-Sud).

How often will you post?+

We post 4-7 times per week across your platforms — a mix of listing posts, neighbourhood content, market stats, and engagement posts to keep you visible daily.

How long does setup take?+

3-5 business days. We audit your social presence, set up automation, install retargeting pixels, create your content calendar, and start posting.

Is there a long-term contract?+

No long-term commitment required. We offer flexible month-to-month plans because the engagement metrics and leads speak for themselves.

Will the content be bilingual?+

Yes. All content is created in both French and English to reach the full Montreal and Longueuil market. We understand the bilingual nature of the Quebec real estate audience.

What if I'm not satisfied?+

We offer a satisfaction guarantee. If you don't see measurable improvements in engagement and leads within 30 days, we'll optimize your strategy for free — or you can cancel.
